Parish Ride Sharing

Members of our parish helping one another get to church!

This volunteer-led initiative has been established to help connect parishioners who need transportation with those willing to offer a ride, as well as those who simply wish to carpool, share travel expenses, and enjoy fellowship along the way. Participation is optional.

While parish volunteers provide and maintain the sign-up system, all ride arrangements are made directly between participating parishioners. Each participant is responsible for communicating and coordinating with each other regarding scheduling, changes, cancellations, or any other practical matters related to their ride.

Please note: While this effort is under the pastoral oversight of the parish, it is not administered by the clergy or the Saint Andrew parish office. All ride-related arrangements and logistical concerns are the responsibility of the participating parties and should be addressed between each other, rather than directed to the priests or parish office.

St. Andrew Orthodox Church, its clergy, staff and volunteers are not responsible for any arrangements, vehicles, drivers, riders, schedules, costs, injuries, damages or losses. Ride at your own discretion, drive with love and arrange details directly with each other.
